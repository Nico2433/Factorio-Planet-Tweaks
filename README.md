# Nayfall Planet Tweaks

A compatibility mod that balances and tweaks interactions between various planet mods.

## Features

### [Planet Castra](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/castra?from=search)

**Works with: [Metal and Stars](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/metal-and-stars?from=search)**

- Physical ammo productivity changed to heavy ammo productivity
- Railgun ammo productivity removed (now part of heavy ammo)
- Rocket ammo productivity removed (uses Castra explosive ammo instead)
- Bullets productivity moved to Castra

### [Cerys](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Cerys-Moon-of-Fulgora?from=search)

**Works with: [Secretas&Frozeta](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/secretas?from=search)**

- Cerys modules removed (Secretas has equivalent modules)
- Similar to [xor's planet tweaks and compat](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/xor_planet_tweaks_compat?from=search)

### [Corrundum](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/corrundum?from=search)

- Fuel productivity technology removed
- Corrundum recipe added to vanilla fuel productivity

### [Igrys](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Igrys?from=search)

**Works with: [Planet Maraxsis](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/maraxsis?from=search)**

- Maraxsis glass productivity removed
- Maraxsis glass added to Igrys glass productivity

**Works with: [AAI Industry](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/aai-industry?from=search)**

- Glass added to Igrys glass productivity

### [Janus](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/janus?from=search)

- Time distorters now have module slots
- Productivity enabled for intermediates, science packs (except replication), and afterburner thruster fuel
- New time-shifted inserters (bulk and stack) added
- Unlocked with time-shifted inserters technology

**Works with: [Muluna, Moon of Nauvis](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/planet-muluna?from=search)**

- Afterburner thruster fuel added to thruster fuel productivity research

### [Metal and Stars](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/metal-and-stars?from=search)

- Basic magazines and shotgun ammo added to bullet productivity

**Works with: [Muluna, Moon of Nauvis](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/planet-muluna?from=search)**

- Space fuel productivity research removed (Muluna already have one)

**Works with: [Cupric Asteroids](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/cupric-asteroids?from=search)**

- Smart rounds added to bullet productivity research

### [Planet Paracelsin](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Paracelsin?from=search)

**Works with: [Moshine](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Moshine?from=search)**

- Solar panels and accumulators replaced with Paracelsin versions
- Paracelsin structures have 1/4 stats (4 structures = 1 Moshine structure)
- Same space efficiency as original
- Similar to [xor's planet tweaks and compat](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/xor_planet_tweaks_compat?from=search)

### [Planet Rubia](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/rubia?from=search)

**Works with: [Secretas&Frozeta](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/secretas?from=search)**

- Rubia efficiency module 4 removed (Secretas has equivalent module)
- Similar to [xor's planet tweaks and compat](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/xor_planet_tweaks_compat?from=search)

### [Shattered Planet](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/skewer_shattered_planet?from=search)

- Duplicated tier 3 modules removed
- Replaced with vanilla modules

### Ammo Compatibility

**Works with: [OCs Ammo and Armor](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/OCs_ammo_casting?from=search)**

- All ammo recipes added to respective ammo productivity research

**Works with: [Cannon Turret](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/vtk-cannon-turret?from=search)**

- All ammo recipes added to respective ammo productivity research

## About

This mod fixes duplicates, balances productivity research, and ensures smooth compatibility between different planet mods.
